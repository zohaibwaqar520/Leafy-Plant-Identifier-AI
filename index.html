Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is Leafy's AI plant recognition? +
Leafy uses trained vision models benchmarked over 30,000+ flora species, achieving over 98% accuracy. Taking photos from multiple angles (leaves, stem, whole plant) further increases diagnostic precision.
Can Leafy identify plant diseases and pests? +
Yes! Leafy detects powdery mildew, black spot, leaf scorch, blight, rust, spider mites, aphids, thrips, and root rot, providing immediate organic healing and recovery steps.
How do the watering care reminders work? +
You can set flexible watering frequencies (e.g. Every 3, 7, or 14 days). Leafy schedules local system alarms directly on your device to notify you when your plant is thirsty.
Is my personal data and photo history safe? +
Leafy values user privacy above all else. Your checkup history is stored securely on your device, and we never sell your personal data or photos. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
